MY STORY & MISSION

Why I Build Games and Apps

I grew up playing games and always dreamed of making my own. When I see apps like Duolingo starting small and growing into companies that create jobs for thousands of people, it motivates me deeply.

My goal as a solo entrepreneur is not just to build software. I want to earn my own living, grow, and one day give back to help small talented creators and underprivileged people achieve their potential too.

"I will never put microtransactions or pay-to-win tricks in my games. My games are free. Yes, I have ads so I can keep building, but I respect players first."

Building alone is tough, and the reality is that indie projects take time to take off. But I believe if you build with honesty and keep improving based on real player feedback, good people will notice and support the journey.

🚫 No Microtransactions

No paywalls or predatory capitalism tactics. Full games free for all players.

💬 Built On Feedback

Tell me what you love and what you hate so I can improve every update for you.

🤝 Bigger Purpose

Working hard to create opportunities and help other talented, humble creators.
